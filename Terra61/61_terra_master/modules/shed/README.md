# Victorian railway shed module

A six-metre repeatable iron-and-glass train-shed bay with two perimeter columns, open lattice arches, roof glazing, slender glazing bars and four suspended lanterns. The central volume contains no supports or walls.

## Files

- `train_shed_bay.glb`: self-contained repeatable structural bay, 8,084 triangles.
- `train_shed_end_frame.glb`: self-contained optional terminal arch and two columns, 3,032 triangles; use to support the last edge of a run.
- `shed_single_preview.png`: a CPU render of the bay GLB.
- `shed_repeat_preview.png` and `shed_interior_preview.png`: CPU renders of three actual bay GLBs and one terminal frame. The neutral background, floor grid and soft grounding shadows appear only in the preview.
- `generate_shed.py`: reproducible mesh, texture and GLB generator.
- `render_preview.py`, `shed_renderer.cpp`: CPU renderer that reads the actual GLB geometry and embedded textures. Its lighting and transparency are approximations, not a specific game engine's PBR output.
- `validate_shed.py`, `validation.json`, `manifest.json`: verification script and data.
- `ai_aged_cast_iron.png`, `iron_plain.png`, `iron_riveted.png`, `aged_brass.png`, `roof_glass.png`, `lantern_glass.png`: editable source textures, all also embedded in the applicable GLB.

## Assembly

Coordinates are in metres, with **Y up** and ground at Y = 0. The bay's bounds are X −9.99 to 9.99, Y 0 to 12.071 and Z 0 to 6. The leading primary arch is centred on Z = 0.28 m; the lighter intermediate arch is centred on Z = 3.13 m. The final edge is supported by the next repeated frame.

For *N* bays place bay origins at `(0, 0, 6*i)`, for `i = 0 … N−1`. To support the open end, place `train_shed_end_frame.glb` at `(0, 0, 6*N−0.28)`; its arch then lies at the end of the roof. The end frame intentionally has a little depth on either side of that joint.

The lowest surface crossing the sample track envelopes X −5.8 to −2.2 and X 2.2 to 5.8 is at Y 9.324 or higher; the X −1.5 to 1.5 central corridor is clear up to Y 10.847. These are conservative triangle bounding-box checks across the full module depth, not a physics simulation. A 4.5 m tall train fits within the tested envelopes. No track, platform, terrain or collision proxies are included.

## Materials and origin

The included `ai_aged_cast_iron.png` is the generated base-color image supplied by the assembly lead. Its exact PNG bytes are embedded in both GLBs and UV mapped onto the main arch chords and column cores. The remaining texture images are originally generated by `generate_shed.py` (NumPy random seed 8126). Fine surface texture and rivets are represented in material maps; the arches, columns, glazing bars, bosses and lanterns are geometry.

The roof is a separate RGBA-textured material using glTF `alphaMode: BLEND` and `doubleSided: true`, with about 26% image alpha. Preserve the alpha mode, texture alpha and double-sided setting in the destination engine; transparent rendering and sorting depend on that engine. The glass is a single thin curved surface, not a thick solid wall. Lantern glass uses an emissive factor, but actual point lights are not included.

## Rebuild and checks

From this directory, with Python, NumPy, Pillow and pygltflib installed:

```sh
python generate_shed.py
python validate_shed.py
python render_preview.py
```

`render_preview.py` also needs `g++` and `trimesh` is required by the validator. Generation reads a copy of the AI iron texture in this folder; if the shared project material exists it first refreshes that local copy. Each GLB loads in trimesh and passes the checks supplied by pygltflib's provisional validator. The custom validation checks triangle data, face degeneracy, bounds, track clearance, texture integrity, roof transparency flags and byte-for-byte inclusion of the AI iron image. These checks are not a replacement for a target-engine import or an official Khronos glTF Validator run.
