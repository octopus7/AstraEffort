# Victorian passenger carriage

A single reusable burgundy passenger carriage with gold-lined timber bodywork, 11 glazed windows on each side, a curved metal roof, two two-axle bogies, spoked wheels, and buffer/coupling details at both ends. The simple interior has an aisle floor and two rows of seat cushions and backs. It is designed to be seen through the windows, not as a fully detailed walkable interior.

## Files

- `victorian_carriage.glb`: complete self-contained model, 13,484 triangles; all base-color images are embedded.
- `carriage_hero_preview.png`, `carriage_side_preview.png`, `carriage_end_preview.png`: renders of the exported GLB and embedded textures. The neutral floor and backdrop are preview only.
- `generate_carriage.py`: reproducible geometry, texture and GLB source.
- `render_preview.py`, `carriage_renderer.cpp`: CPU renderer used for these views; its lighting and transparency approximate rather than reproduce a particular game engine's PBR rendering.
- `validate_carriage.py`, `manifest.json`, `validation.json`: checks and measured output.
- The supplied PNG files are editable copies of the textures embedded in the GLB.

## Placement and proportions

Coordinates are metres with Y up. Actual local bounds are X −1.41 to 1.41, Y 0 to 4.12 and Z 0.006 to 15.194 m (including buffers and couplings). Total dimensions are approximately **2.82 × 4.12 × 15.19 m**. Wheel treads reach Y = 0 and the axle planes are at Z 2.60, 4.20, 11.00 and 12.60 m. Wheel centres lie at X ±0.78 m; the inner tread edges are near X ±0.70 m.

Place the carriage's local origin at the track centreline and use **Y = +0.24 m** above the station's ground datum, as specified by the assembly. The highest point then reaches Y = 4.36 m, inside a 4.5 m envelope. Repeat along Z with an approximately 15.2 m pitch for touching couplers; adjust for any desired gap. This model is static: wheels and bogies are geometry, not separately rigged or animated parts. Track, station and collision proxies are not included.

## Materials and texture sources

The AI-generated `ai_aged_cast_iron.png` supplied by the project is embedded byte-for-byte and UV mapped onto the underframe and wheel/bogie iron. The other base-color textures are generated by `generate_carriage.py` with NumPy random seed 40617. The letter decal uses DejaVu Serif Bold, installed with the environment, to render “RIVERDALE RAILWAY.” The simulated wood grain, gold, glazing, upholstery and metal textures are procedural and original.

Window panes are actual openings in the body with a separate double-sided RGBA `BLEND` material, not painted pictures. Preserve the texture alpha and transparency mode on import; display and sorting vary by target engine. Gold lining and the major frame details are modeled, while surface grain and signs use UV texture. The roof surface is opaque metal.

## Rebuild and verification

In this folder, with Python, NumPy, Pillow, pygltflib and trimesh:

```sh
python generate_carriage.py
python validate_carriage.py
python render_preview.py
```

`render_preview.py` also needs `g++`. The generator reads the AI texture from the shared project material folder when available, otherwise from its local copy. The validation script independently reloads the GLB with trimesh, runs pygltflib's provisional checks, checks bounds and zero-area triangles, confirms the transparent window material and all embedded PNGs, compares the AI image bytes, checks wheel contact heights, and fully decodes all preview PNGs. These checks are not an official Khronos glTF Validator run or an import test in the target engine.
