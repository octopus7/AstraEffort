# Victorian platform and freight props

Three independent, low-polygon, textured props for a railway platform. Each GLB has embedded materials and image maps, is in metres with Y up, and has its local origin at ground height.

| Model | Size X × Y × Z | Triangles | Pivot |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `platform_sign.glb` | 1.22 × 3.11 × 0.38 m | 1,066 | Centre of the post base, with the sign hanging toward +X |
| `cargo_crate.glb` | 1.134 × 1.1225 × 1.134 m | 548 | Centre of the crate footprint |
| `cargo_barrel.glb` | 0.86 × 1.04 × 0.86 m | 288 | Centre of the barrel footprint |

The sign has an iron pedestal, bracket, hanging panel, and a cream numeral **3** on both sides. The crate has actual frame pieces, diagonal braces and thin corner plates. The barrel has a sixteen-sided bulged silhouette, a closed plank lid and four iron hoops. Wood grain, plank seams, numeral, pin heads and rivets are image details rather than high-density meshes.

The image files `platform_sign_preview.png`, `platform_sign_back_preview.png`, `wooden_crate_preview.png` and `wooden_barrel_preview.png` are CPU renders of the actual GLB meshes and their embedded images. Their neutral background, floor grid and grounding shadow are preview-only. The renderer approximates PBR lighting and is not a target-engine screenshot.

## Materials and sources

`platform_3.png`, the gold color, the wood plank and lid textures, and the painted rivets were generated by `generate_props.py`; the procedural wood uses NumPy seed 110213 and the sign numeral uses the DejaVu Serif Bold font. The iron images are reduced or decorated derivatives of the project's AI-generated `materials/ai_aged_cast_iron.png`. A 512-pixel intermediate, `iron_ai_small.png`, is included here so the assets can be regenerated without the original shared material. PNGs in the folder are editable copies; all images needed to display each model are already inside its GLB.

## Build and checks

With Python, NumPy, Pillow, pygltflib and trimesh installed:

```sh
python generate_props.py
python validate_props.py
python render_preview.py
```

The preview renderer also needs `g++`. The validation checks the actual GLB data, zero-area faces, image data, Y=0 placement, UV-mapped opposing sign faces, and imports each model into trimesh; it also runs pygltflib's provisional checks and decodes the preview PNGs. The models have not been run through the official Khronos glTF Validator or tested in a particular game engine.
