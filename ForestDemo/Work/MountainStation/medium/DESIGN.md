# Alder Pass — MountainStation / medium

Started 2026-10-21 12:27:10 UTC / 21:27:10 KST. User approved independent execution and design discretion; no additional design approval or delegated art work.

An original 100 × 100 m mountain railway stop. Warm plaster, teal standing-seam roofs, aged timber, muted greenery and grey rock form a restrained palette. The station occupies the near side of a gently bending railway. Its curved platform coping follows the same curve as the rails. A dark horseshoe tunnel sits at the east end, backed by faceted mountain outcrops. A covered freight deck anchors the opposite end of the station group. A west ramp provides access from the forecourt, and a separate hillside trail climbs toward the rear.

Original props include slatted benches, luggage with handles, analog clock, station signs, lantern posts, strapped crates, fences, conifers and stones. No faces, hearts or decorative cloud motifs. Geometry is authored in a private background Blender process, imported into the private UE project and given independently authored UE materials.
