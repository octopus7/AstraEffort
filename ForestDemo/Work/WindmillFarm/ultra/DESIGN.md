# Old Windmill Farm — independent scene

Authority: ASSIGNMENT.md. Autonomous design and execution are already approved. This work does not resume Forest or enter the comparison experiment.

The scene is a 100 by 100 metre quiet farm in late afternoon. A weathered, maintained windmill stands just behind a broad working courtyard; its pale tapered tower and four timber-and-linen sails are the landmark. A low barn and tool shed frame the right side. Crop strips and a fenced vegetable garden occupy the foreground, with orchard trees, split-rail fences and soft grass at the perimeter. Curving dirt paths connect the entrance, mill door and barn without blocking pedestrian access.

Palette: sage and olive greens, warm grey stone, desaturated ochre plaster, weathered walnut wood, faded terracotta roofing, off-white sail cloth. Rounded bevels, visible joinery, stone courses, wheel spokes, modest wear and small planted details provide form rather than saturated colors. No faces, hearts, decorative clouds or imported creative assets.

Production: independent background Blender 4.5 processes create owned FBX sources with UVs. New deterministic textures and Unreal materials are authored in this job. A new content-only private UE5.7 project imports and assembles only /Game/WindmillFarm/ultra assets. Moving sails are optional polish only if a safe owned Blueprint/material implementation can be validated; static sails are an acceptable disclosed limitation.
