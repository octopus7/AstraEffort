# Sansol Springs / 산솔 온천

Create an original, quiet 100 x 100 m hillside village, with three inhabited terraces, two irregular stone pools, restrained cedar and plaster buildings, folded tile roofs, a continuous gentle switchback approach, alternate stairs, a small gravity-fed watercourse, and sparse rounded vegetation. South is the arrival side; north rises into the wooded hill. Architecture and paths must visibly touch their supporting ground.

The central outdoor bath is the focal point, with a smaller upper pool. Muted sage, warm limestone, cedar brown, indigo roof tiles, and mineral turquoise water are the palette. Warm small lamps are accents, not a nighttime spectacle. No faces, hearts or decorative clouds.

All geometry and textures will be authored afresh in this task. Separate UV charts use measured area-based density; tiling surface materials and intentional overlaps are documented. Water uses an animated shader. Steam uses restrained animated translucent meshes/cards: an artistic approximation, not fluid simulation. Screenshots must be actual Unreal renders.

Only the assigned HillsideOnsen ultra Content, map, Work and ignored scratch paths may be written. Minimal unthemed baseline runtime support may be copied read-only. No other scene sources or creative resources are read or reused. No shared project changes, Git mutations, comparison accounting, or old report edits.

This is a new scene project. The user explicitly authorized autonomous design and implementation without further approval gates. Use direct implementation with independent technical validation review. Report actual limitations rather than inventing test results.
